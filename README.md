# SmartBuff Forever

**Client:** World of Warcraft: Forever

SmartBuff helps you keep the important buffs up on yourself, your party or raid, and eligible pets — with reminders, one-click casting, and per-template profiles suited to Solo, Party, Raid, Battlegrounds, and named Forever/Classic raids.

## What it does

SmartBuff watches your group and inventory, then reminds you (chat, splash, and/or sound) when buffs, consumables, or conjures are missing or about to expire. You can cast from the action button, scroll wheel, target change, or a keybind. Options are available with `/sbm`.

## Features

- **Self, party, and raid buffing** — class and role filters (Tank / Healer / Damage), plus Additional and Ignore lists per buff
- **Pets** — optional Hunter and Warlock pet buffing
- **Consumables** — flasks, elixirs, food, scrolls, and Forever alchemy items where configured
- **Conjure / create items** — mage food, water, and gems; warlock stones (skips when bags are full or the product is already present)
- **Weapon buffs** — oils, sharpening stones, weightstones, poisons (class-dependent)
- **Templates** — Solo, Party, LFG, Raid, Battleground, Arena, plus named instance profiles (Classic Era and Forever raids such as Molten Core, Naxxramas, Hyjal Summit, The Barrow Deeps)
- **Auto template switching** — by group type and optionally by instance name
- **Reminders** — splash message, chat, and LibSharedMedia splash sounds
- **Combat options** — limited in-combat cast sequence for flagged buffs; normal out-of-combat buffing otherwise
- **Paladin assignments** — per-class blessings and auras, shared with other paladins in your party or raid
- **Forever-focused** — Classic Era content plus Forever-specific pieces (e.g. Skyborne racials); not for Retail or other Classic flavors

## Paladin assignments

Paladins set one aura and one blessing per class. Greater blessings are used when you know them; otherwise the single blessing is used. Seals, hands, and other self buffs stay on the normal buff list.

Open the window with `/sb paladin` or `/smartbuff paladin`, or Shift-click the minimap button. It can also open from the options button, by clicking a blessing or aura in the buff list, or automatically when you join a party or raid. Anyone can view the window. Any paladin can change an unlocked row, including another paladin’s. Tick Lock to keep your own row from being changed. Hover a class icon for a short hint about which blessings usually fit that class.

## Usage

| Command / binding | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `/sbm` | Open the options menu |
| `/sb paladin` | Open or close Paladin assignments |
| `/smartbuff paladin` | Open or close Paladin assignments |
| Left-click minimap | Options |
| Shift-click minimap | Open or close Paladin assignments |
| Right-click minimap | Enable / disable |

Configure buffs per template, enable the reminder, and use the action button, scroll wheel, or keybinds to cast.
